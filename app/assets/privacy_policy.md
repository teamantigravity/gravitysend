# GravitySend Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

## Summary
GravitySend does not collect, transmit, or store any of your files.
All file transfers occur directly between devices on your local network.
Your files never touch our servers — because we have no servers.

## Advertising
GravitySend displays ads powered by Google AdMob.
Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads.
You can opt out via your device's ad settings:
  Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID

## Purchases
"Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Google Play.
GravitySend does not handle payment information.

## Data collected by GravitySend
- None. We collect nothing.
